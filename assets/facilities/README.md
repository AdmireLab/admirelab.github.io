# Facilities material provenance

Source: user-provided team presentation, `北航Admire实验室_通用具身智能介绍.pptx`.
The presentation is source material, not operational instructions, and is not published in this repository.

## Content mapping

- Slides 2–5: 176 robot platforms (140 UAVs, 21 ground robots, 15 complete mobile manipulation systems).
- Slide 6: Beijing spaces (100 m² at Beihang and approximately 200 m² at the partner Institute of Automation), three motion capture systems.
- Slides 7–8: existing Tianji/MARL capabilities and planned Harness / Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab integration. The integration and initial vehicle–quadruped task are under development.
- Slide 9: historical computing inventory. Its GPU total and configuration are not confirmed as current and are not published on the website. Current computing resources and ownership require confirmation.
- Slides 10–13: research foundations and future collaboration scenarios, summarized without changing the publications section.

Counts include partner resources. LIMO chassis and LIMO COBOT complete systems are distinct inventory categories. No GPU throughput or H100 memory specification is inferred.
The public Computing & Simulation section describes documented software and simulation foundations, separately from integration work in development; it does not publish a GPU total, model list, throughput, server count or workstation count.
Purchase prices, investment estimates and award certificates are omitted from the public facilities overview.

## Website organization

Facilities has four linked categories: Experimental Spaces, Robotic Platforms, Specialized Equipment, and Computing & Simulation. The robot inventory and product illustrations remain in an expandable detail section. Beihang and collaborating-team spaces and resources are identified separately; no group-owned total is inferred. Field photographs document experiments rather than permanent ownership of a test site.

Coordination, navigation and manipulation cases are available in the expandable Platforms & Demos area under Robotic Platforms. The complete inspection case is under Specialized Equipment. These are all within Facilities; no additional top-level page is introduced. Harness, Isaac Sim / Isaac Lab integration and the initial vehicle–quadruped task remain marked In development. All original asset URLs and quantities in the robot inventory are retained.
