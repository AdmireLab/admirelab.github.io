# Facilities research assets

Source materials supplied by the team:

- `北工大具身硬件基础.pptx`, slides 1-2: CARLA cockpit, EEG acquisition setup and OpenBCI descriptions. Slide 3: gimbal hardware and airshow integration.
- `吊舱成果.pptx`, slide 1: gimbal stabilization animation, image augmentation and feature-matching diagrams, and engineering results. Slide 2: flight inspection, defect detection, statistics, assistant and report animations.

The full gimbal and inspection case is available in an expandable area under Facilities → Specialized Equipment, alongside its hardware introduction. CARLA and EEG equipment retain their BJUT and collaborating-laboratory attribution, separately from the robot inventory. No computing equipment quantities are added. Certification and award claims are omitted because the specific holder is not established by the currently available public material.

Photographs and diagrams are converted to WebP without cropping. The EEG photograph is rotated upright. Five GIF animations are served as animated WebP, and the gimbal stabilization sequence is served as an H.264 MP4. Each uses a separate static poster. The full sequences and playback durations are retained. Media loads only after the visitor opens the image lightbox or video player; closing the viewer unloads it. Text embedded in source diagrams and recordings remains in its original language.

The source PPTX files are not published. Original photographs, diagrams, recordings, posters and media URLs are preserved. Static illustrations, five animated WebPs, and the MP4 stabilization video are labeled according to their actual media type. The MP4 duration is 00:44, rounded from 43.667 seconds.

The original gimbal animated WebP remains available in Git history.
